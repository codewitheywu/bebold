Our team is at the heart of everything we do

Each member of the BeBold Recovery team brings years of experience, unwavering dedication and a genuine passion for helping people in early recovery. Together we create a supportive, empowering environment where every client feels seen, heard and valued.

That means your care is never handed off to a stranger. From your first call with our admissions coordinator through discharge planning, the same small group of Virginia-licensed professionals knows your name, your history and your goals — and meets weekly to review your progress in our PHP and IOP programs.
